Joanne Rowling, known by her pen name J. K. Rowling, is a British author and philanthropist. She is the author of Harry Potter, a seven-volume fantasy novel series published from 1997 to 2007. Wikipedia
Born: July 31, 1965 (age 59 years), Yate, United Kingdom
Children: Jessica Isabel Rowling Arantes, Mackenzie Jean Rowling Murray, David Gordon Rowling Murray
